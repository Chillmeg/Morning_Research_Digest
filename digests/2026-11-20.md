## 2. AGSR — Aperture-Guided Super-Resolution for Gaussian Splatting

**Source:** arXiv [2611.25934](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25934) · [ATHENA lab page (AAU Klagenfurt)](https://athena.itec.aau.at/2026/07/6631/)
**Authors:** Ayman Alkhateeb, Hadi Amirpour, Christian Timmerer
**Date:** Submitted to arXiv Nov 19, 2026. Accepted at IEEE MMSP 2026, where it won Best Paper.

**What it is:** An add-on that runs at inference time on an already-trained 3DGS model. It renders the scene at higher resolution by contracting projected 2D covariances and rendering through multiple "apertures", then fuses the results with a tiny network of at most 66K parameters, in real time.

**What's new:** It needs no retraining and no image-space SR network that hallucinates detail. The method works inside the splat rasterizer. On Mip-NeRF 360 it beats lightweight image-space SR baselines, and the margin grows as the Gaussian representation gets coarser or more compressed.

**Relevance to your work:** It pairs naturally with compressed or low-budget splats for XR streaming: train and ship a small model, then upsample at display time on high-PPD headsets.

## 3. CryoGaussian — Heterogeneous Cryo-EM Reconstruction with Gaussian Splatting

**Source:** arXiv [2611.26128](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26128)
**Date:** Listed as submitted Nov 19, 2026. *I couldn't confirm this beyond a search snippet, so check the abstract page.*

**What it is:** A method that fits an explicit Gaussian density representation directly to cryo-EM particle images and models *heterogeneous* structures, meaning molecules in multiple conformations. Earlier work such as CryoSplat / CryoGS ([2508.04929](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04929)) and GEM handled the homogeneous case.

**What's new:** It extends splatting from rendering photographic scenes to tomographic inverse problems with conformational variability. In effect this is a "dynamic / deformable 3DGS" applied to biology.

**Relevance to your work:** It's a sign that 3DGS is becoming a general differentiable volumetric primitive. The deformation and heterogeneity modeling ideas may carry over to dynamic-scene 4DGS, and the reverse may also hold.

## 4. Kosmo-GFM — A Production-Ready Geometry Foundation Model

**Source:** [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/KosmoResearch/Kosmo-GFM) · [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/papers/kosmo-gfm-a-production-ready-geometry-foundation-model) · [AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/kosmo-gfm-rebuilds-3d-scene-capture-from-sparse-photos-26x-faster)
**Authors:** Kosmo Research
**Date:** Recent, appearing in this week's aggregators. I couldn't confirm the exact release date.

**What it is:** A system in the VGGT / D4RT family with three parts:
- **GFM:** predicts cameras, depth, correspondences and dynamic 3D points from images or video, in 1.65–1.77B-parameter variants including a 4D variant.
- **PoseEngine:** turns those predictions into scene-level poses for 3DGS and 4DGS.
- **KosmoWorld:** a data engine that re-renders reconstructed scenes to make more supervision.

**What's new:**
- A single camera-aware attention backbone handles **pinhole, fisheye and equirectangular** inputs.
- It reports the best depth AbsRel and pose AUC@5° on the SpatialBench Sparse and Medium subsets.
- Coverage claims sparse-photo capture is about 26× faster.
- Only inference code is public. PoseEngine and the full GS pipeline are not released.

**Relevance to your work:** It could replace COLMAP for initializing splats, especially with 360° and fisheye rigs common in XR capture. It's worth benchmarking against VGGT-Ω and Depth Anything 3 on your own data.

## 5. Topo-4DGS + TopoEgo — Topology-Aware 4D Gaussian Splatting for Egocentric Video

**Source:** arXiv 2611.16012 · [Radiance Fields listing](https://radiancefields.com/papers/topology-aware-4d-gaussian-splatting-for-egocentric-videos)
**Authors:** Leonardo Vanni, Stefano Gasperini, Federico Tombari, Chiara Plizzari
**Date:** November 2026

**What it is:** TopoEgo is a benchmark of egocentric interactions that change topology: fission, fusion, opening and closing. Topo-4DGS is a method that gives interacting parts their own rigid transforms and switches Gaussians on or off as surfaces appear, disappear or separate.

**What's new:** Standard 4DGS deforms one fixed set of Gaussians, so it fails when someone cuts, opens or splits an object. Topo-4DGS beats state-of-the-art 4DGS methods, especially on frames after the topological event. The authors say code and data will be released.

**Relevance to your work:** This is directly relevant to reconstructing hand-object interaction from headset video for XR, robotics and training data. TopoEgo also fills a gap among 4DGS benchmarks.

## 9. MatrixSplat — Matrix-Parallel, Feature-Scalable 3DGS Rendering

**Source:** arXiv [2611.15153](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15153)
**Date:** Nov 11, 2026. Accepted to NeurIPS 2026, which is just under three weeks away, so this is timely context.

**What it is:** A GPU rendering framework that recasts per-pixel feature accumulation in 3DGS as matrix operations on **Tensor Cores**. Rendering then scales well to high-dimensional per-Gaussian features such as semantic, language or neural features.

**What's new:** Rasterizers like the original 3DGS and gsplat are CUDA-core bound, and their cost grows linearly with feature dimension. MatrixSplat targets that bottleneck.

**Relevance to your work:** It's useful for feature or semantic splatting (LangSplat-style), where feature rendering is the bottleneck.

## Emerging Trends This Week

1. **4D splats are becoming practical.** A benchmark (TopoEgo), a capture method (Open4DGS), a file format (GSAF) and a viewer (SuperSplat 3.9) all arrived in the same week.
2. **Geometry foundation models are being productized.** Kosmo-GFM follows VGGT-Ω, Depth Anything 3 and D4RT, with more attention to camera models, 4D and deployment.
3. **Splatting is spreading beyond graphics.** Recent examples include cryo-EM (CryoGaussian), wireless channel modeling ([2611.01122](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01122)) and enterprise GIS (Esri).
4. **Rendering at inference time and on hardware.** AGSR (super-resolution at render time) and MatrixSplat (Tensor Cores) both get more out of splats without retraining.
