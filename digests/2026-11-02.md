## 1. GradSplat — Gradients Inside the Forward Pass for Feed-Forward 3DGS
**Source:** [arXiv 2610.38951](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38951) · **Date:** submitted Oct 28, 2026

**What it is:** A feed-forward Gaussian splatting model that refines its own Gaussians with learned gradient steps inside a single forward pass. It reports the best PSNR/SSIM among nine methods on six-view RealEstate10K.
**What's new:** Brings optimisation-style refinement into feed-forward inference without a per-scene optimisation loop.
**Relevance:** A candidate drop-in for fast sparse-view scene capture where per-scene 3DGS training is too slow.

## 2. CompactSplat — Spatially Adaptive Gaussians for Feed-Forward Reconstruction
**Source:** [arXiv 2610.13177](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.13177) · **Date:** Oct 2026

**What it is:** Allocates Gaussians adaptively by scene content to reduce redundancy in sparse-view feed-forward reconstruction.
**What's new:** Reduces Gaussian counts without a fixed per-pixel budget.
**Relevance:** Smaller splat sets help memory and streaming for XR delivery.

## 3. Feed-Forward Multi-View Feature Coding for 3DGS (MFGS)
**Source:** [arXiv 2610.20590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20590) · **Date:** submitted Oct 14, 2026

**What it is:** An end-to-end feed-forward method that encodes multi-view features into a bitstream, rather than compressing explicit Gaussians.
**What's new:** Shifts compression from the primitives to the features that generate them.
**Relevance:** Relevant to transmitting captured scenes to lightweight XR clients.
