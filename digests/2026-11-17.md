## 1. World Labs Atlas: Open to All Developers

**Source:** [Auganix](https://auganix.org/xr-news-world-labs-atlas-world-model-apps-api) · [Hermes AI](https://hermes-ai.net/news/world-labs-opens-atlas-3d-world-model-to-all-developers/) · [The Decoder (original Atlas announcement)](https://the-decoder.com/world-labs-unveils-atlas-a-single-ai-model-that-generates-reconstructs-and-simulates-3d-worlds-from-just-a-few-photos/) · [World Labs blog](https://worldlabs.ai/blog/bigger-better-worlds)
**Date:** Reported Nov 16, 2026 (Atlas first announced Sep 1, 2026)

**What it is:** World Labs has moved Atlas, its "omni world model" pretrained on text, images, video and 3D, out of closed partner access. Two web apps launched with it: **Viewfinder**, which reconstructs scenes from images, and **Chisel**, which is a geometry-first authoring tool. The API is reportedly self-serve, and worlds can be exported as keyframes, video, and **3D Gaussian splats**.

**What's new:** Until now Atlas could only be used through partner agreements. One model that does both *generation* and *reconstruction* from a few photos is now publicly available, and it exports splats.

**Why it matters to you:** It can serve as a ready-made baseline or data generator for sparse-view reconstruction and scene completion. The splat export means its output can go straight into existing 3DGS renderers and XR viewers. It is worth benchmarking Viewfinder against your own few-view 3DGS pipelines. *Caveat:* the access and export details come from secondary outlets, so check World Labs' own docs before relying on them.

## 2. Vauss: Source-Available Video→Splat Trainer (Voyager)

**Source:** [Radiance Fields news feed](https://radiancefields.com/) · [Brush platform page](https://radiancefields.com/platforms/brush)
**Date:** Nov 16, 2026 (research preview)

**What it is:** Vauss is a Rust trainer and command-line tool built on the open-source **Brush** 3DGS trainer. It turns a single video into a Gaussian splat and exports it for a web viewer, Blender, a voxel game format, and Unity. The Brush-derived training crates stay under Apache 2.0. The new Vauss code uses a separate "Vauss Preview License".

**What's new:** It covers the whole path from a phone video to a splat inside a game engine or DCC tool in one cross-platform tool. Usually that takes COLMAP, a trainer and per-engine converters chained together.

**Why it matters to you:** Vauss could make a quick capture-to-XR prototyping path, especially next to Blender 5.3's native splat support (see the past-week section). Read the preview license before using it in published research code, because only the Brush-derived parts are Apache-2.0. The Unity export format has not been documented yet.

## 3. IGRecon4D: Dynamic-Object-Aware Feed-Forward Reconstruction of Driving Scenes

**Source:** [arXiv 2611.21126 (HTML)](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.21126) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.21126)
**Date:** arXiv v1, Nov 16, 2026 (cs.CV)

**What it is:** A feed-forward method that predicts Gaussian primitives for driving scenes in a single pass and models dynamic objects explicitly instead of treating them as noise. The authors evaluate on the Waymo Open Dataset and report gains over feed-forward baselines.

**What's new:** Most feed-forward 3DGS and pointmap models in the VGGT and DUSt3R lineage assume static scenes. IGRecon4D handles moving objects without per-scene optimization, which pushes feed-forward reconstruction toward 4D.

**Why it matters to you:** Any feed-forward 4DGS work you do on dynamic capture could reuse its method for separating dynamic objects. That includes human or object motion in XR capture, not just driving. Compare it with D4RT (CVPR '26 Best Paper, covered in the June 12 digest) as a non-Gaussian 4D baseline.

## Past Week: Worth Knowing

### Gaussian Splatting / 3D Reconstruction Research
- **Stage-wise Lineage-aware Dropout for Sparse-View 3DGS** ([arXiv 2611.17752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17752), Nov 13): reduces overfitting in sparse-view 3DGS by applying dropout over "lineage families", which are groups of Gaussians descended from the same initial primitive. It is a cheap regularizer you could add to few-view pipelines.
- **Topo-4DGS + TopoEgo benchmark** ([arXiv 2611.16012](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16012), Nov 11): a benchmark and method for *topological changes* in egocentric video, such as cutting or opening objects. Most 4DGS deformation fields cannot handle these changes. It is directly relevant to egocentric XR capture.
- **MatrixSplat** ([arXiv 2611.15153](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15153), Nov 11, NeurIPS 2026): maps splat feature accumulation onto Tensor Cores so high-dimensional *feature* rendering runs faster. This helps semantic and language-embedded splatting, such as distilling features into splats.
- **Camera3R** ([arXiv 2611.13453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13453), Nov 10): LoRA adaptation that extends perspective-trained feed-forward reconstruction networks to fisheye and other camera models. This matters for headset and 360° cameras.
- **Perception Over Geometry: Gaussian allocation in mesh-based splatting** ([arXiv 2611.07979](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07979), Nov 5): allocates Gaussians by how much appearance detail a surface needs, rather than by mesh geometry alone.

### Tools
