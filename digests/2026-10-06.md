## 1. PocketSplat — Mobile Gaussian Reconstruction via World-Space Latent Allocation

**Source:** [arXiv 2610.03192](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03192)  
**Authors:** Wenzhi Guo, Xianda Chen, Dongxuan Chen, Guangchi Fang, Bing Wang et al.  
**Date:** Submitted Oct 2, 2026

**What it is:** A feed-forward framework that builds Gaussian assets under an explicit budget: dense latent candidates are organised in predicted world space, an exact integer capacity is allocated per local cell, and full Gaussian attributes are decoded only for retained candidates. Cell-conditioned fusion aggregates multi-view evidence before decoding.

**What's new:** It runs directly on a target iPhone and, on Mip-NeRF 360, produces compact, higher-quality assets substantially faster than a deployable streamed MVSplat variant. Most feed-forward splat models assume server GPUs and produce per-pixel Gaussians with no size control.

**Why it matters to you:** On-device, size-controlled splat capture is the missing piece for phone-to-XR pipelines (capture, preview, and share without cloud round trips).

## 2. Budgeted-GS — Real-Time Large-Scale Gaussian Splatting via Factoring LOD

**Source:** [arXiv 2610.03162](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03162)  
**Author:** Haipeng Wang (preprint targeting EG 2027)  
**Date:** Submitted Oct 2, 2026

**What it is:** A post-hoc layer that converts any trained 3DGS model into a "factoring tree", a multi-resolution hierarchy of moment-matched aggregate Gaussians. A single quality parameter picks, per view, the level of detail that fits the target GPU's memory. Construction takes a few seconds. The same theory can be applied during training ("budget-centered training") to size the model before optimisation.

**What's new:** LOD for 3DGS usually needs retraining or bespoke hierarchical pipelines. This works on existing checkpoints, so one city-scale model can serve GPUs of very different capacity.

**Why it matters to you:** Pairs naturally with large-scale renderers (e.g. the Gaussian Point Splatting work in the June digest) for streaming big scenes to memory-limited XR devices. It is cheap to try on models you already have.

## 3. MEGA — Object-Level Mesh Extraction from 3DGS via Spatial Visual Distillation

**Source:** [arXiv 2610.01707](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01707)  
**Authors:** Liwei Liao, Yingkui Zhang, Qianqian Tong, Ronggang Wang  
**Date:** Submitted Oct 1, 2026

**What it is:** A "segment-then-mesh" framework that extracts object-level, watertight meshes from complex 3DGS scenes, using Spatial Visual Distillation and a mask-guided neural surface reconstruction module.

**What's new:** Prior mesh-from-3DGS methods work at scene level and often yield non-watertight surfaces. MEGA reports state-of-the-art object occupancy and demonstrates combining meshes (for physics/occupancy) with splats (for rendering).

**Why it matters to you:** Watertight per-object meshes make captured splat scenes usable for collision, physics and editing in XR and game engines, and bridge 3DGS capture with conventional 3D modeling workflows.

## 4. ABot-Recon — Revisiting Local Context for Long-Horizon Streaming 3D Reconstruction

**Source:** [arXiv 2608.27529](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27529) · [GitHub](https://github.com/amap-cvlab/ABot-Recon) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/acvlab/ABot-Recon)  
**Authors:** Jiarong Han, Jincheng Xiong, Yuzhou Liu et al. (AMAP CV Lab)  
**Date:** Submitted Aug 27, 2026 (not new this week; included because code and weights are public and it is still surfacing in recent results)

**What it is:** A streaming model with a strictly local 12-frame temporal window (current frame plus KV features from the previous 11) that recovers global poses and geometry from reference-frame-consistent local predictions, with a lightweight temporal rotation refiner and a composition-aware pose loss.

**What's new:** Bounded memory and compute regardless of sequence length. On Oxford Spires it reports ATE 4.35 m and RPE-R 0.12°, about 40% lower error than the best prior results.

**Why it matters to you:** A practical feed-forward alternative to SLAM-style pipelines for long captures, and a candidate source of poses and geometry to initialise Gaussian splatting on long videos.

## 5. Meta "VR Glasses" — Puck-Tethered Mixed Reality Headset

**Source:** [UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/meta-vr-glasses-officially-announced-connect-2026/) · [Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/meta-vr-glasses-unveiled-price-release-date/) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2267218/meta-vr-glasses-price-specs-apple-vision-pro-comparison/)  
**Date:** Announced Sep 23, 2026 (Connect); on sale Spring 2027 at $1,300

**What it is:** A ~100 g head unit with micro-OLED panels (5.5 MP per eye, 37 PPD, up to 120 Hz), pancake lenses, eye tracking, and 26 PPD colour passthrough. Compute (Snapdragon "Reality Elite") and a battery (3+ h claimed) live in a ~300 g puck on an optical cable. Hand and eye tracking are the primary inputs; no controllers are bundled.

**What's new:** A Vision Pro-class display and passthrough in a much lighter form factor, from the largest consumer XR platform.

**Why it matters to you:** It sets the likely next hardware target for passthrough and spatial apps: high pixel density means more demanding splat/mesh rendering budgets, and eye tracking enables foveated rendering for large scenes. This is older than 24 hours, so it is context rather than breaking news.

## Trends to Watch

1. **Budget-aware 3DGS** — PocketSplat (build-time) and Budgeted-GS (render-time) both treat Gaussian count as a first-class, controllable constraint, aimed at mobile and consumer-GPU deployment.
2. **Splats to usable geometry** — MEGA continues the push to turn photoreal splat scenes into interactive, physically meaningful assets.
3. **Bounded-memory feed-forward reconstruction** — streaming models with local context (ABot-Recon) are replacing global-attention approaches for long sequences.
