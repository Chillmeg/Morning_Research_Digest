## 1. Framestore's Superman 4D Splatting Pipeline and TuttiCam

**Source:** [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/) · **Date:** Oct 23, 2026

**What it is:** Framestore published details of the 4D Gaussian splatting pipeline used on *Superman*. Alongside it, TuttiCam, a free iOS app with an open-source Android version, can sync up to eight phones for multi-device 3DGS capture.

**What's new:** A major VFX studio documenting a production 4D splatting pipeline is a strong signal of adoption in film. Commodity multi-phone sync lowers the cost of dynamic capture.

**Why it matters to you:** It is a practical reference for dynamic-scene capture workflows, and TuttiCam offers a cheap way to collect multi-view video datasets for 4D reconstruction experiments.

## 2. CHCNAV CoCloud Adds a 3DGS Rendering Engine

**Source:** [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/) · **Date:** Oct 23, 2026

**What it is:** CHCNAV's CoCloud platform gained a Gaussian splat renderer that reportedly preloads models up to 5 GB in seconds.

**What's new:** Survey and geospatial vendors are shipping splat viewing as a standard product feature, with large-model load times as a selling point.

**Why it matters to you:** It shows large-scale splat delivery and streaming becoming a product requirement. That is relevant to XR map and digital-twin experiences.

## 3. Rivian to Acquire Third Dimension AI (SuperSim)

## 4. Motion- and Gravity-Aware Feedforward 3D Reconstruction

**Source:** [arXiv 2610.22177](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22177) · **Date:** Submitted Oct 15, 2026

**What it is:** A feed-forward reconstruction model that incorporates motion and gravity cues, aimed at dynamic scenes where appearance-only transformer attention struggles.

**What's new:** It adds physical priors to feed-forward geometry models. Related October work, [PURE (arXiv 2610.14698)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14698), ACM MM 2026, refines feed-forward reconstruction with unlabeled post-training to address domain shift.

**Why it matters to you:** Both target the known weakness of feed-forward models on dynamic or out-of-distribution scenes, which matters for casual-video capture for XR. The [survey of feed-forward 3D scene modeling (arXiv 2604.14025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14025v1) was revised Oct 10 and is a good map of the field.

## Trends to Watch

1. **Splatting is moving from research to production pipelines:** film (Framestore), geospatial (CHCNAV), and infrastructure inspection (MLIT).
2. **Feed-forward reconstruction is turning to robustness:** physical priors, post-training and stress tests aim to fix dynamic-scene and domain-shift failures.
3. **Reconstruction for simulation is attracting acquisitions** (Rivian / Third Dimension AI).
