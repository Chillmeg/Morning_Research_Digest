## 1. GrapeSplat — Pose-Free Feed-Forward 3D Gaussian Splatting

**Source:** [arXiv 2609.23182](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.23182)
**Date:** arXiv ID indicates a late-September 2026 submission.

**What it is:** GrapeSplat ("Geometry-Grounded Reconstruction via Amalgamated Pose-Free Encoding") is a feed-forward 3DGS method that does not need known camera poses. Only the title was visible to me.

**What's new:** It continues the pose-free feed-forward line (e.g. PF3Plat, VGGT-style geometry backbones). The title suggests it combines several geometry encoders into one representation.

**Why it matters to you:** Pose-free feed-forward splatting removes the COLMAP step for casual capture. That is the workflow most relevant to XR scene capture from phone video. Read the paper before relying on any claim beyond the title.

## 2. GeoCond — Conditioning-Aware Reliability Adapter for Feed-Forward 3D Reconstruction

**Source:** [arXiv 2609.18465](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.18465)
**Date:** September 2026 (arXiv ID).

**What it is:** An adapter that adds a notion of input "reliability" to feed-forward reconstruction models such as the DUSt3R/VGGT family. Only the title was visible.

**What's new:** Feed-forward reconstructors usually treat all input views equally. A conditioning-aware adapter points toward more robust behaviour on poor or ambiguous inputs.

**Why it matters to you:** Robustness on imperfect captures is the main gap between benchmarks and real XR capture. Check whether the adapter is plug-in for existing checkpoints.

## 3. "Is Gaussian Splatting Becoming Neural Again?" — Taxonomy and Controlled Study

**Source:** [arXiv 2609.12395](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12395)
**Date:** September 2026 (arXiv ID).

**What it is:** A taxonomy of how recent 3DGS systems use neural networks to generate or share Gaussian parameters. It is organised along five axes: attribute decoding, spatial sharing, view-conditioned decoding, topology generation and amortized inference. It analyses 19 representative methods and includes a controlled study.

**What's new:** It is a synthesis paper, not a new method. It gives a shared vocabulary for the drift from explicit splats toward neural or hybrid parameterisations.

**Why it matters to you:** Useful as a map of the design space, and as a source of controlled comparisons if you are choosing between explicit and neural-decoded Gaussians for compression or streaming.

## 4. Nokia Streams Dynamic Gaussian Splats at IBC 2026

**Source:** Reported in search results summarising [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/) coverage.
**Date:** IBC 2026 (September 2026).

**What it is:** A demo of dynamic Gaussian splats delivered through standard video infrastructure: MPEG V3C and GS4 coding, MP4 storage, DASH delivery, rendered on Samsung and XREAL devices.

**What's new:** Splat video moving onto standards-based streaming stacks, rather than custom players.

**Why it matters to you:** Standardised splat codecs and delivery are a prerequisite for volumetric video on XR glasses. I saw only the summary, so bitrates and quality figures are unknown.
