## 3. Feed-Forward 3D Gaussian Splatting: Compactness and Refinement

**Sources:** [PhGS (arXiv 2609.20623)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.20623) · [F⁴Splat](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-37602-2_1) · [VolSplat (ECCV 2026 poster)](https://eccv.ecva.net/virtual/2026/poster/3169) · [FlexSplat](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.07937) · [Saliency-Guided Primitive Merging](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.10712)
**Date:** Aug–Sep 2026 (PhGS arXiv ID indicates Sep 2026)

**What it is:** A cluster of recent work on feed-forward 3DGS. PhGS does post-hoc pruning and refinement of single-view feed-forward Gaussian reconstructions. F⁴Splat predicts per-region densification scores so the Gaussian budget can be controlled explicitly. VolSplat uses voxel-aligned rather than pixel-aligned prediction (ECCV, Sep 10).

**What's new:** The emphasis is shifting from "can feed-forward predict splats" to making them compact, budget-controllable and refinable — addressing the redundancy of per-pixel Gaussians.

**Why it matters to you:** Compact, controllable feed-forward splats are what on-device XR capture and streaming need. Voxel-aligned and budgeted predictions are good candidates for baselines.
