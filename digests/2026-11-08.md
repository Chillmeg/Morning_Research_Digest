## 1. OctreeSplat
**Source:** [arXiv 2611.02658](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02658) · **Date:** Nov 2, 2026
**What it is:** A feed-forward Gaussian splatting method that uses an octree to allocate primitives adaptively, plus stochastic solids volume rendering for better depth and surface reconstruction.
**What's new:** Pixel-aligned feed-forward methods produce redundant Gaussians; hierarchical allocation cuts counts while keeping quality.
**Relevance:** Compact outputs help with XR/mobile deployment, and the improved depth helps mesh extraction.

## 2. Nuke 18.0 open beta
**Source:** [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/tags/gaussian-splatting) · **Date:** Oct 27, 2026
**What it is:** Foundry's compositor adds splat viewport optimization, a FieldThreshold node for procedural splat isolation, and splat selection/snapping.
**What's new:** Splats become editable, selectable assets inside a mainstream VFX pipeline.
**Relevance:** Signals splat cleanup/editing moving into production tools.

## 3. Splat Tour
**Source:** [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/tags/gaussian-splatting) · **Date:** Oct 22, 2026
**What it is:** Builds browser property tours (360 panoramas, floor plan, optional splat walkthrough) from one phone video per floor.
**What's new:** Consumer-grade capture-to-splat web delivery without specialist hardware.
**Relevance:** A reference for casual-capture pipelines and web-based splat viewing.

## 4. Anchor3DGS
**Source:** [arXiv 2610.21634](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21634) · **Date:** Oct 15, 2026
**What it is:** Pose-free feed-forward 3DGS that replaces pixel-aligned prediction with a compact anchor-based representation (NeurIPS 2026 submission).
**What's new:** Removes the need for known camera poses while shrinking the representation.
**Relevance:** Complements OctreeSplat; both point to compact, pose-free feed-forward reconstruction as the active trend.
