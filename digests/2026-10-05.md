## Research (most recent found; not confirmed within 24h)

### 4. VS-Splat — voxel-selective feed-forward Gaussian splatting (Sep 2026)
**Source:** [arXiv 2609.12343](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12343)
End-to-end 3D object reconstruction from sparse views using a feed-forward Gaussian predictor with voxel-based selection of Gaussians.
**Relevance:** Continues the trend of removing per-scene optimization; useful for fast object capture from few images.

### 5. RIGOR — rig-informed geometry for omnidirectional reconstruction (Sep 2026)
**Source:** [arXiv 2609.13504](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.13504)
Uses known multi-camera rig geometry to improve omnidirectional 3D reconstruction.
**Relevance:** Relevant to 360° / rig-based capture for XR scene digitization.

### 6. Revisiting Local Context for Long-Horizon Streaming 3D Reconstruction (Aug 2026)
**Source:** [arXiv 2608.27529](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.27529)
Addresses streaming reconstruction over long sequences, where drift and memory limit feed-forward geometry models.
**Relevance:** Long-sequence, online reconstruction for mapping and AR.

### 7. Trend: feed-forward Gaussian splatting keeps accelerating
**Sources:** [ATSplat (arXiv 2607.20417)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.20417) · [AnchorSplat (arXiv 2604.07053)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07053)
Recent work focuses on compact token-based and geometry-prior-anchored feed-forward predictors rather than pixel-aligned Gaussians.
