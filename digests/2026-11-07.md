## 1. CaliSplat
**Source:** [alphaxiv 2611.04413](https://alphaxiv.org/de/abs/2611.04413) — submitted Nov 3, 2026
Image compression using sparse Gaussian fitting with calibrated rate allocation. Reported 3.0×–15.7× less per-image fitting time than representative Gaussian codecs with competitive or better rate-distortion.
**New / useful:** Fitting cost is the main obstacle to Gaussian-based codecs; this makes them more practical, and may carry over to compact splat representations generally.

## 2. Efficient partial updates for 3DGS
**Source:** [Bioengineer.org](https://bioengineer.org/partial-updates-bring-faster-3d-gaussian-splatting-scene-changes) — reported Nov 4, 2026
Updates only the changed parts of a Gaussian scene when the real world changes, instead of retraining from scratch, with theoretical analysis.
**New / useful:** Relevant to maintaining living digital twins and large captured scenes (e.g. XR maps) cheaply.

## 3. Real-time Gaussian digital humans
**Source:** [Altii.de](https://altii.de/gaussian-splatting-forscher-machen-digitale-menschen-detailreicher) — preprint dated Nov 2, 2026
Korean group reports more detailed faces and clothing at ~34 FPS using ~2 GB GPU memory (benchmark datasets, not peer-reviewed).
**New / useful:** Suggests avatar fidelity within reach of XR-class hardware budgets; verify claims once code/paper is available.

## 4. FLEG (ECCV 2026)
**Source:** [Embodied-AI-Daily, Nov 6](https://github.com/luohongk/Embodied-AI-Daily/issues/1075)
Feed-forward method building language-embedded 3D Gaussians from multi-view images.
**New / useful:** Combines feed-forward reconstruction with open-vocabulary semantics — useful for queryable scenes in XR. Same roundup series ([Nov 1](https://github.com/luohongk/Embodied-AI-Daily/issues/1068)) lists FreeView (reconstruction from uncurated views) and VGGT-Ω (CVPR 2026 oral).
