## 1. Autodesk 3ds Max 2027.3: Gaussian splats in the viewport, 4DGS playback, PLY export

**Source:** [CG Channel](https://www.cgchannel.com/?p=180392) · [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/3ds-max-2027-3-adds-splat-viewport-display-and-4dgs) · [MoguraVR](https://www.moguravr.com/?p=381681)
**Date:** ~November 19, 2026

**What it is:** 3ds Max 2027.3 adds a viewport splat shader that sorts splats back to front within each object and aims to match renderer colors. It can play back a numbered sequence of PLY/SPZ files as an animated **4D Gaussian splat** on the timeline, and it can export splat objects as PLY, with up-axis and unit options. The release also includes a **3ds Max MCP server** (tech preview) that LLM agent clients can drive.

**What's new:** Release 2027.2 (July) made splats an editable native object. 2027.3 closes the loop with real-time viewport display, time-varying (4DGS) assets and export back out. Splats can now go **into and out of** a mainstream DCC tool as first-class geometry.

**Why it matters to you:** Captured or generated 3DGS and 4DGS (e.g., from dynamic reconstruction pipelines) can now be checked, edited and laid out alongside meshes in a production tool, then exported as PLY. That makes it a practical evaluation and authoring target for research outputs. The MCP server also makes agent-driven scene assembly a real option.

## 2. PaveSplat: resolution-robust feed-forward Gaussian splatting ⚠️

**Source:** [arXiv 2611.26045](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26045)
**Date:** v1 submitted November 19, 2026 (per listing; accepted to ACCV 2026)

**What it is:** A feed-forward 3DGS method that predicts a **learned residual on top of the analytic surface-aligned covariance** for each Gaussian. The goal is to keep reconstructions stable when inference resolution differs from training resolution.

**What's new:** Pixel-aligned feed-forward 3DGS models are known to break when input resolution changes, because Gaussian scales are tied to pixel footprints. Grounding covariances in surface geometry and adding only a learned correction is a principled fix, and it sits alongside recent work (SplatAnything, InfiniSplat) that also moves away from pure pixel alignment.

**Why it matters to you:** If you deploy feed-forward reconstruction on varied capture devices or resolutions (phones, headsets, drones), this is a cheap architectural change worth testing. ⚠️ The arXiv ID looks unusually high for its date. Check the listing before citing it.

## 3. MatrixSplat: matrix-parallel, feature-scalable 3DGS rendering on GPUs

**Source:** [arXiv 2611.15153](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15153) · [NeurIPS 2026 poster](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/poster/152933)
**Date:** arXiv mid-November 2026; NeurIPS 2026 (early December)

**What it is:** A tile-based 3DGS renderer that maps per-pixel feature accumulation onto **warp-level MMA operations on Tensor Cores**. It adds warp-granularity Gaussian packing and a lazy, channel-batched feature-fetch pipeline that overlaps memory access with compute.

**What's new:** Standard 3DGS rasterizers leave Tensor Cores idle, and their cost grows roughly linearly with feature dimension. The authors report up to **3.8× over 3DGS**, **2× over TC-GS**, and up to **36.7× lower render-kernel time for high-dimensional features**, with an extra 1.69–2.84× when the method is dropped into existing fast renderers. Evaluation covers Mip-NeRF 360, LERF and 3D-OVS.

**Why it matters to you:** Feature splatting (semantic, CLIP or DINO features for open-vocabulary 3D understanding) has been held back by rendering cost. This makes language-embedded and semantic 3DGS far more practical, including for interactive XR scene querying.

## 5. Android XR adds OpenXR Spatial Container support

**Source:** [UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/android-xr-gets-spatial-container-support-to-enable-multi-engine-apps/)
**Date:** November 16, 2026

**What it is:** Android XR now supports the newly standardized **OpenXR Spatial Container extensions** on devices with Spatial API Level 4 or higher, so apps can add 3D volumes to 2D apps and mix engines. It currently works only for apps in Full Space mode.

**What's new:** This is a standards-based (OpenXR) route to multi-app spatial composition. UploadVR notes it still trails visionOS and Pico OS 6 multitasking.

**Why it matters to you:** For XR research prototypes, it is a cross-vendor way to embed 3D content, such as a splat viewer, next to conventional UI without writing to a proprietary API. The Android XR glasses wave (XREAL Aura, and Warby Parker / Gentle Monster glasses shipping December 10) makes the platform more relevant as a target.

## 6. ArcGIS XR Viewer beta (November 2026): Gaussian splat layers in XR

**Source:** [Esri ArcGIS Blog](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/arcgis/3d-gis/whats-new-in-xr-viewer-beta-november-2026) · [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/arcgis-xr-viewer-adds-initial-gaussian-splat-layer-support)
**Date:** November 2026

**What it is:** Esri's XR Viewer beta (Early Adopter Program) adds initial support for **Gaussian splat layers**, along with 3D point layers, a voice assistant and VR sketching.

**Why it matters to you:** Geospatially referenced splats viewed in headsets is a real enterprise use case for 3DGS in XR (digital twins, site inspection). Together with Cesium's LoD 3D Tiles splats and the glTF splat extension, it shows splats becoming a standard GIS data type. That is useful for anyone working on large-scale or georeferenced reconstruction.

## Context: important items from earlier this month

### 7. Blender 5.3 ships native Gaussian splats (Nov 10)
**Source:** [Blender Developers Blog](https://code.blender.org/2026/10/splatting-the-gaussians-into-blender/) · [Learn Architecture](https://learnarchitecture.net/architecture-news/46953-blender-5-3-gaussian-splats-archviz.html)
Blender now imports splats from PLY, SPZ and glTF natively. Blender says performance is still being optimized. With 3ds Max 2027.3 and Unreal plugins (XGRIDS LCC4Unreal, Volinga 4DGS), **all major DCC tools now handle splats natively**, so open-source research outputs can go straight into artist workflows.

### 8. SplatAnything: Gaussian Spacing Normalization (arXiv Nov 5; code Nov 9)
**Source:** [arXiv 2611.08264](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08264) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/splatanything-releases-inference-code-with-gaussian-spacing-fix)
SplatAnything identifies a normalization mismatch in Gaussian attributes that shifts scale targets and makes supervision ambiguous across scenes, and it fixes this with Gaussian Spacing Normalization. Inference code and weights are out. It is a strong baseline for cross-scene feed-forward 3DGS generalization. Related: InfiniSplat (ZJU3DV, SIGGRAPH Asia 2026 Journal Track), a single-image, surface-aligned feed-forward 3DGS model with [checkpoints on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/PLUS-WAVE/InfiniSplat).

### 9. Google's Oct–Nov model wave: Gemini 4 family and Genie 4
**Source:** [TechCrunch on Genie 4](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/googles-genie-4-world-model-adds-audio-more-realistic-worlds-and-real-time-steering/) · [Gemini (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_(language_model))
Genie 4 (announced Oct 27; research preview, now rolling out to AI Ultra and Gemini API early access) adds native audio and text-based live steering, with Gemini acting as a planner. Google acknowledges that consistency drifts over longer sessions. Gemini 4 Carbon (Nov 10) and Hydrogen (Nov 12) are now stable. Interactive world models are becoming a competitor to explicit 3D reconstruction for generating explorable XR environments. Measuring their 3D consistency against 3DGS ground truth is an open research gap.
