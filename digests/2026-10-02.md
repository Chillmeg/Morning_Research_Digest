## 1. Samsung's first Android XR glasses reportedly launching in November

**Source:** [9to5Google (Oct 1, 2026)](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/01/samsung-android-xr-glasses-release-date-report) · [Droid Life (Oct 1, 2026)](https://droid-life.com/2026/10/01/report-samsungs-first-ai-glasses-coming-in-november)
**Date:** October 1, 2026

**What it is:** Reports say Samsung has confirmed a November launch for its first Android XR smart glasses ("Intelligent Eyewear"). They are display-free, with camera, microphones and speakers, using Gemini via a paired Galaxy phone, and reportedly several grams lighter than Meta's base Ray-Ban (51 g).

**What's new:** A concrete launch window for the first Samsung Android XR glasses; no exact date or price yet.

**Why it matters to you:** Camera-equipped, always-on glasses widen the pool of egocentric capture devices and the Android XR developer ecosystem, which matters for egocentric 3D capture and spatial-AI apps. Being display-free, they are less relevant for rendering/splatting work.

## 3. Streaming feed-forward 3D reconstruction and splatting (recent arXiv)

**Sources:** [Revisiting Local Context for Long-Horizon Streaming 3D Reconstruction (arXiv 2608.27529)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.27529) · [StreamSplat: Streaming Feed-Forward 3D Gaussian Splatting (arXiv 2608.01659)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.01659) · [ATSplat (arXiv 2607.20417)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.20417) · [GenSplatCodec (arXiv 2607.24403)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.24403)
**Date:** July–August 2026 (not within the last 24h)

**What it is:** A continuing wave of feed-forward methods: streaming geometry for long videos, streaming Gaussian prediction for dynamic scenes with persistent Gaussians, compact token-expanding Gaussian predictors, and one-step-diffusion compression of feed-forward splats.

**Why it matters to you:** The trend is from per-scene optimisation toward online, compressed, pose-free pipelines, directly relevant to real-time capture and streaming of splat scenes to XR devices. I did not read these papers in full; check abstracts before relying on claims.
