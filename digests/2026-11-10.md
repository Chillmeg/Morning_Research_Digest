## 1. Unified Loss-Aware Density Control for 3D Gaussian Splatting
**Source:** [arXiv 2611.02044](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02044) — submitted ~Nov 1, 2026 (reported accepted to NeurIPS 2026)
**Summary:** Reframes adaptive density control (clone/split/prune of Gaussians) as local loss-reduction decisions rather than separate heuristics.
**What's new / relevance:** Densification heuristics are a main source of quality and memory variance in 3DGS; a principled, unified criterion could be a drop-in improvement to training pipelines.

## 2. METRIC-GS: Selective Metric-Scale Estimation for 3DGS
**Source:** [arXiv 2611.06020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06020v1) — submitted ~Nov 4, 2026
**Summary:** Addresses the arbitrary scale of SfM-based reconstructions by estimating metric scale selectively, so content can be placed in real-world units.
**What's new / relevance:** Metric scale is required for AR anchoring, measurement and robotics; this closes a practical gap between splat captures and XR use.

## 3. Perception Over Geometry: Rethinking Gaussian Allocation in Mesh-based Splatting
**Source:** [arXiv 2611.07979](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07979) — submitted ~Nov 5, 2026
**Summary:** Studies how Gaussians are allocated across mesh faces, favouring perceptual importance over purely geometric criteria.
**What's new / relevance:** Mesh-bound Gaussians are the bridge to editable, animatable assets; better allocation means higher quality at the same budget.

## 4. SHIN: Scalable Hierarchy for INcremental 3DGS
**Source:** [arXiv 2611.10652](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10652) — submitted ~Nov 7, 2026 (date per search snippet)
**Summary:** A hierarchical representation with a reusable base plus additive enhancement layers, targeting different bandwidth and device budgets.
**What's new / relevance:** Progressive/LOD splats are key for streaming scenes to headsets and mobile XR.
