## 1. 3DGS for Fine-grained Urban Scene Reconstruction

**Source:** [arXiv 2610.16292](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16292) · **Date:** Oct 12, 2026

**Summary:** Adds semantic-guided density control and a new loss design to 3D Gaussian Splatting for urban scenes. Reported PSNR gains are ~2.7–5.2% over vanilla 3DGS.

**What's new / relevance:** Semantic cues drive where Gaussians are densified, rather than purely gradient heuristics. Useful if you work on large outdoor captures where fine structures (facades, signage) are under-reconstructed. Gains are modest and relative to the original 3DGS baseline, so compare against stronger recent baselines before adopting.

## 2. Gaussian Splatting for Real-Time Scene Reconstruction: Methods, Systems, and Trade-offs

**Source:** [arXiv 2610.17513](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17513) · **Date:** Oct 13, 2026

**Summary:** A comparative overview of real-time GS reconstruction methods and systems, discussing trade-offs.

**What's new / relevance:** A survey, not a new method — a convenient map of the field for onboarding or related-work sections, especially on systems/latency trade-offs relevant to XR deployment.

## 3. InfiniSplat — Surface-Aligned Splats (SIGGRAPH Asia 2026, Journal Track)

**Source:** [AI Weekly summary](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/infinisplat-lands-siggraph-asia-2026-with-surface-aligned-splats) (secondary source; verify against the paper)

**Summary:** Feed-forward Gaussian reconstruction that places Gaussians on estimated surfaces (geometry-guided sampling from depth-induced local structure) instead of fixed pixel positions. Reports zero-shot transfer from Hypersim indoor training to open-world scenes; code released.

**What's new / relevance:** Moves away from pixel-aligned Gaussians, a known weakness of feed-forward methods (redundancy, poor geometry). Relevant for fast, pose-light capture pipelines and cleaner downstream meshing.

## 4. AnyGS2Mesh — Feed-Forward Mesh Reconstruction from 3DGS

**Source:** [arXiv 2609.03304](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.03304) · **Date:** Sep 2026

**Summary:** Described as the first feed-forward framework to directly produce meshes from 3DGS representations, supporting arbitrary-resolution views.

**What's new / relevance:** Avoids slow per-scene optimisation in the splat-to-mesh step, bridging splats and conventional 3D modeling/engine workflows. Related feed-forward work this season: [PhGS](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.20623) (post-hoc pruning/refinement of single-view feed-forward reconstructions) and [GrapeSplat](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.23182) (pose-free feed-forward 3DGS).
