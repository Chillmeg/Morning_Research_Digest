## 1. SpatialQ — Understanding 3DGS Scene Quality via Visual-based MLLM

**Source:** [arXiv 2607.26595](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26595) · [HTML version](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26595v1)
**Authors:** Jingxuan Su, Shenglin Wang, Tiesong Zhao, Ge Li, Wei Gao
**Date:** July 29, 2026

**What it is:** SpatialQ is a multimodal quality-assessment framework for 3D Gaussian Splatting scenes. It pairs a spatial quality encoder — which learns scene-level quality representations from multi-view rendered images to produce a base quality score — with a grounded multimodal reasoning module that feeds rendered images, depth maps, point-cloud renderings, and camera parameters into a Qwen-based MLLM.

**What's new:** Prior 3DGS quality metrics (e.g., 3DGS-IEval-15K) score individual rendered views for perceptual fidelity but ignore scene-level structure and cross-view consistency. SpatialQ is one of the first frameworks to reason jointly over geometry, depth, and multi-view appearance to explain *why* a splat scene looks wrong, not just *that* it does.

**Why it matters:** As Gaussian Splatting moves into production pipelines (mapping, virtual production, XR capture — see items 2–4 below), automated, explainable quality control becomes essential for catching capture artifacts before a scene ships. This is a building block for automated QA in any 3DGS content pipeline.

## 2. KIRI Engine Releases Free Gaussian Splatting Plugin for After Effects

**Source:** [CG Channel](https://www.cgchannel.com/2026/07/check-out-this-free-gaussian-splatting-plugin-for-after-effects/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/Kiri-Innovation/Gaussian-Splatting-plugin-by-KIRI-Engine) · [KIRI Engine blog](https://www.kiriengine.app/blog/gaussian-splatting-after-effects-workflow)
**Date:** July 30, 2026

**What it is:** A free, MIT-licensed After Effects plugin from 3D-scanning company KIRI Innovation that renders Gaussian Splat `.PLY` captures directly through AE's native 3D camera — with creative controls for color, crop, opacity, scale, noise, displacement, and density.

**What's new:** Previously, using a 3DGS capture inside a motion-graphics/VFX compositing pipeline meant pre-baking it to flat video. This plugin lets splats be framed, animated, and art-directed live inside AE alongside other 3D layers, and it's free and open-source (MIT).

**Why it matters:** Lowers the barrier for motion designers and VFX artists to bring photogrammetry-free, real-world captures into standard production pipelines — another sign that Gaussian Splatting tooling is maturing from research code into everyday content-creation software.

## 3. NVIDIA vkSplatting 2026.1 — Ray-Traced Shadows and Multi-Instance Splats

**Source:** [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/nvidia-releases-vulkan-gaussian-splatting-2026.1) · [SplatLabs](https://www.splatlabs.ai/blog/nvidia-vulkan-gaussian-splatting-2026) · [GitHub (nvpro-samples)](https://github.com/nvpro-samples/vk_gaussian_splatting)
**Date:** Recent release, showcased at NVIDIA GTC 2026 (Apache 2.0, open source)

**What it is:** NVIDIA's reference Vulkan renderer for 3D Gaussian Splatting adds a multi-instance splat-set architecture (splats can be instanced, indexed, and reused across a scene via global index tables and unified sorting), proper multi-light support (point/spot/directional) with ray-traced hard and soft shadows, front-to-back rasterization with depth consolidation, deferred shading for splats, a centralized bindless asset system, and DLSS Ray Reconstruction for denoising/upscaling.

**What's new:** Earlier 3DGS renderers largely treated splats as a standalone, unlit representation. vkSplatting 2026.1 pushes splats toward parity with conventional real-time rendering pipelines — dynamic lighting, shadows, and scene composition — rather than a single frozen radiance capture.

**Why it matters:** Relighting and multi-object composition are prerequisites for using Gaussian Splatting in game engines and interactive XR content rather than just static walkthroughs. This is a strong signal that splats are being engineered toward general-purpose real-time graphics, not just photorealistic captures.

## 4. Khronos glTF Gaussian Splatting Extension — Ratification Still Pending

**Source:** [Khronos press release](https://www.khronos.org/news/press/gltf-gaussian-splatting-press-release) · [GitHub spec](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF/blob/main/extensions/2.0/Khronos/KHR_gaussian_splatting/README.md) · [CG Channel](https://www.cgchannel.com/2026/02/3d-gaussian-splats-are-being-added-to-the-gltf-standard/)
**Date:** Release candidate published Feb 3, 2026; ratification targeted for Q2 2026 but not yet finalized as of Aug 2, 2026

**What it is:** `KHR_gaussian_splatting` defines how Gaussian Splat attributes — position, orientation, scale, spherical-harmonic color, and opacity — are stored inside glTF mesh primitives, enabling any glTF-compatible engine or viewer to load splat scenes without a proprietary format.

**What's new:** This would be the first vendor-neutral interchange format for Gaussian Splats, similar to what glTF already provides for meshes. The extension has been in release-candidate/community-feedback status since February; as of today it has missed its original Q2 target, worth watching for final ratification.

**Why it matters:** Standardization removes the current fragmentation across `.ply`/`.splat`/proprietary formats and would let splat content move freely between DCC tools, engines, and XR runtimes — directly relevant to any pipeline distributing splats across multiple platforms (e.g., visionOS RealityKit, Android XR).

## 5. SAM 3D: 3Dfy Anything in Images — CVPR 2026 Best Paper Honorable Mention

**Source:** [CVPR 2026 Best Papers](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2026/News/Best_Papers) · [BasicAI breakdown](https://www.basic.ai/blog-post/cvpr-2026-top-papers-award-winners-and-notable-works)
**Authors:** Meta Superintelligence Labs
**Date:** Award reported late July 2026 (CVPR 2026)

**What it is:** SAM 3D is a generative model that reconstructs visually grounded 3D geometry, texture, and scene layout from a single natural image — predicting per-object shape and texture, then estimating a coherent multi-object layout, rather than requiring multi-view input.

**What's new:** This CVPR 2026 Honorable Mention sits alongside D4RT (this year's Best Paper, covered in our prior digest) as one of the conference's top 3D-reconstruction results, and pushes single-image-to-3D-scene reconstruction — historically a weak point for feed-forward methods — into best-in-class territory.

**Why it matters:** Single-image scene reconstruction with plausible multi-object layout is directly useful for populating XR environments or generating 3D assets from casual photos, without requiring a structured capture session.

## 7. Zyphra Zamba2-VL — Hybrid Mamba2/Transformer Vision-Language Models

**Source:** [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/12/zyphra-release-zamba2-vl-hybrid-mamba2-transformer-vision-language-models-that-cut-time-to-first-token-by-about-an-order-of-magnitude/) · [Zyphra](https://www.zyphra.com/our-work/zamba2-vl)
**Date:** June 2026 (still an active reference point in current CV-model discussion)

**What it is:** A 1.2B/2.7B/7B family of open, Apache-2.0 vision-language models built on the Zamba2 hybrid backbone — Mamba2 state-space layers carrying most of the compute, interleaved with a small number of shared transformer attention layers.

**What's new:** Because prefill is largely linear-time with a fixed-size recurrent state, Zamba2-VL cuts time-to-first-token by roughly an order of magnitude versus similarly sized Transformer VLMs, while staying competitive on chart/diagram/document understanding, general perception, and counting benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** For latency-sensitive CV applications (real-time scene captioning, on-device XR assistants), a VLM family with drastically lower first-token latency at comparable quality is a meaningful architectural alternative to dense-Transformer VLMs.

## Emerging Trends to Watch

1. **Gaussian Splatting tooling is consolidating into standard pipelines.** KIRI Engine's After Effects plugin, NVIDIA's vkSplatting relighting/shadow support, and the pending glTF standard all point toward 3DGS becoming a first-class citizen in mainstream DCC and game-engine workflows rather than a research-only representation.
2. **Quality assurance for splats is now its own subfield.** SpatialQ joins prior quality-assessment work (3DGS-IEval-15K) — as splat capture scales to production, automated QA tooling is becoming necessary infrastructure.
3. **XR hardware strategy is bifurcating.** Lenovo's pivot away from standalone XR toward tethered AI wearables, contrasted with Android XR's expanding standalone/glasses device wave at AWE, suggests two competing near-term form factors rather than one converging platform.
4. **Efficient architectures are reaching vision-language models.** Zamba2-VL's SSM/Transformer hybrid approach cutting first-token latency by an order of magnitude suggests non-Transformer backbones are becoming practical for real-time, on-device multimodal use cases — relevant to latency-constrained XR/robotics applications.
