## 1. Mac-native Gaussian Splat training arrives (Sep 30)

**Source:** [Radiance Fields — Gaussian Splatting in September 2026](https://radiancefields.substack.com/p/gaussian-splatting-in-september-2026) · [Gaussian Splatting This Week (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xtu-6sL7Ygs)

**Summary:** On Sept 30, three tools shipped training on Apple Silicon: MetalSplat v0.3.0 (3DGS and 2DGS via hand-written Metal kernels), LuluSplat 1.1 (MCMC trainer plus an MCP server), and Spirula Studio v2026.

**What's new / why it matters:** Until now serious 3DGS training was effectively CUDA-bound. Metal-native 2DGS/MCMC training makes local experimentation possible on laptops, and the MCP server in LuluSplat means splat pipelines can be driven by agents. Useful for quick capture-to-splat iteration without a GPU box.

## 2. Gausser 1.0.0 brings splats to Cinema 4D (Oct 1)

**Source:** [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/) · [Gaussian Splatting This Week](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xtu-6sL7Ygs)

**Summary:** Gausser 1.0.0 lets Cinema 4D drive imported PLY, SPLAT and SPZ splats with MoGraph Fields, Effectors, Deformers and Forces.

**What's new / why it matters:** Splats become procedurally animatable assets in a mainstream DCC tool rather than static captures — relevant for editing/animating reconstructed scenes and for XR content authoring.

## 3. Splats go mainstream across the 3D software stack (September 2026)

**Source:** [Radiance Fields — September 2026 roundup](https://radiancefields.substack.com/p/gaussian-splatting-in-september-2026) · [OpenUSD 26.03 splat support](https://3dvf.com/en/openusd-v26-03-released-an-announcements-that-confirms-the-rapid-rise-of-gaussian-splats/) · [Cesium 3D Tiles splat LOD](https://cesium.com/blog/2026/04/27/3d-gaussian-splats-lod/)

**Summary:** Blender 5.3 added native splat import/rendering; three.js r186 ships a splat renderer with raycasting; Babylon.js put splats in its frame graph; Open3D added splat rendering; GeoLibre streams splat tilesets.

**What's new / why it matters:** Together with earlier OpenUSD and Cesium 3D Tiles support, splats now have a viable interchange/streaming/viewing path. This is a month-level trend, not a 24-hour item, but it lowers the cost of deploying your reconstruction results in web and XR viewers.

## 4. XR market: standalone headsets down, glasses up

**Source:** [Hololight — AR Headsets in 2026](https://hololight.com/news/ar-headsets-in-2026-building-an-xr-strategy-for-a-fast-moving-market) · [Fudzilla](https://fudzilla.com/xr-headsets-lose-their-shine/)

**Summary:** Standalone headset shipments (Quest 3, Vision Pro class) are forecast to fall ~15% in 2026 to ~4.7M, while tethered XR glasses are forecast to grow ~19% to ~900k. Android XR (Samsung Galaxy XR) continues to expand.

**Why it matters:** Suggests target-platform planning should favour lightweight glasses and Android XR/OpenXR-compatible pipelines; forecast figures, not new announcements.

## 5. ML: Claude Sonnet 5.5 and falling reasoning costs

**Source:** [LLM News (Oct 2026)](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news) · [AI Model Releases tracker](https://digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-model-releases-october-2026-tracker)

**Summary:** Sonnet 5.5 is reported faster and cheaper than Sonnet 5; reaching 75% on ARC-AGI-1 reportedly now costs about a cent per task versus ~$26 19 months ago.

**Why it matters:** Cheaper strong models make agentic tooling around reconstruction/vision pipelines (data triage, code gen, evaluation) more practical.
