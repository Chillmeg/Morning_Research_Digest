## 1. OmniFabric — Coherent UV-Space Texture Synthesis for 3D Garment Reconstruction

**Source:** [arXiv 2609.30234](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30234) · [Project page](https://humansensinglab.github.io/OmniFabric/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/humansensinglab/OmniFabric)
**Authors:** Ding-Jiun Huang, Yuanhao Wang, Cheng Zhang, Hugo Bertiche, Alexandru-Eugen Ichim, Thabo Beeler, Fernando De la Torre — Carnegie Mellon University, University of Washington, Texas A&M University, Google
**Date:** Submitted September 28, 2026; accepted to SIGGRAPH Asia 2026 (Dec 1–4, Kuala Lumpur)

**What it is:** Given in-the-wild photos of a garment, OmniFabric reconstructs a simulation-ready 3D garment mesh with a texture map synthesized directly in the 2D sewing-pattern (UV) space, rather than baked onto the estimated 3D surface. It combines a mesh estimator with VLM generative priors and a diffusion transformer trained via an automated synthetic-data engine.

**What's new:** Prior single-image garment reconstruction methods bake lighting, shadows, and wrinkles from the input photo directly into the texture, producing assets that look wrong once re-lit or re-draped in simulation. By refining the texture in the canonical UV domain, OmniFabric removes those distortions and artifacts, producing textures that are reusable across poses and lighting conditions.

**Why it matters:** Simulation-ready, distortion-free garment assets are a real bottleneck for populating XR avatars, virtual try-on, and game/film pipelines from casual photos. The UV-space refinement trick is a pattern that generalizes beyond garments to any UV-parameterized 3D asset pipeline (props, characters) where existing photogrammetry/Gaussian-splat-to-mesh workflows currently bake in unwanted lighting.

## 2. GAPS — Generative Active Pseudo-View Selection for Sparse-View 3D Gaussian Splatting

**Source:** [arXiv 2609.23436](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23436)
**Authors:** Hongfei Zhu, Haochen Deng, Sitao Zhang, Ling Zhou
**Date:** Submitted September 20, 2026; revised September 24, 2026

**What it is:** A framework that augments sparse-view 3DGS training with diffusion-generated pseudo-views. A pre-trained image diffusion model, constrained by a depth-conditioned ControlNet, IP-Adapter style transfer, LoRA scene adaptation, and img2img structural anchoring, synthesizes additional geometrically-consistent supervision views. An alternating "generative active" selection step picks which pseudo-views to add next, balancing how informative a view is against how reliable the generator is for it.

**What's new:** Sparse-view 3DGS is notorious for floaters, broken geometry, and washed-out backgrounds because the optimization is badly under-constrained. Rather than a single round of view augmentation, GAPS actively selects *which* pseudo-views to generate and add, iterating training and generation together — addressing the failure mode where blindly adding generated views reinforces the generator's own hallucinations.

**Why it matters:** Sparse-capture 3DGS (a handful of phone photos, not a full multi-view rig) is exactly the regime most consumer/XR capture tools operate in. An active-selection loop that keeps hallucination artifacts in check without a dense capture is directly applicable to mobile scene-capture pipelines and AR object digitization.

## 3. OVIE — "One View Is Enough!" Monocular Training for In-the-Wild Novel View Generation

**Source:** [Hugging Face paper page](https://huggingface.co/papers/2603.23488) · [Project page](https://kyutai.org/blog/2026-04-14-ovie/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/kyutai-labs/ovie)
**Authors:** Kyutai Labs
**Date:** Accepted to NeurIPS 2026; surfaced in today's arXiv listing update (v3, Sep 24 2026)

**What it is:** OVIE trains a novel-view generator entirely on unpaired, uncurated internet images (30M of them) — no multi-view or pose-annotated data required. At training time only, a monocular depth estimator lifts a source image to 3D, applies a random camera transform, and projects it to get a pseudo-target view; a masked loss formulation ignores disoccluded regions this creates.

**What's new:** At inference, OVIE is fully geometry-free — no depth estimator, no explicit 3D representation — yet it outperforms prior in-the-wild baselines in zero-shot novel view generation while running roughly 600× faster than the next-best method.

**Why it matters:** A geometry-free, single-forward-pass novel-view generator that's this fast is a strong candidate as a fast preview/completion module ahead of (or in place of) heavier 3DGS/NeRF optimization — e.g., generating a plausible alternate viewpoint instantly for AR object placement or scene-capture guidance, before committing to full reconstruction.

## 4. A Neural Hierarchical-Matrix Preconditioner for Real-Time GPU Solves

**Source:** [arXiv 2605.13343](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13343)
**Date:** SIGGRAPH Asia 2026 Posters (Dec 1–4, Kuala Lumpur); listed in today's arXiv digest

**What it is:** A neural preconditioner ("Hierarchical Transformer Preconditioner") for iterative linear solves, structured around a weak-admissibility ℋ-matrix partition (dense diagonal blocks plus coarsened off-diagonal tiles). It approximates the matrix inverse via low-rank far-field factors, with transformer highway connections propagating context across scales, and reduces each PCG iteration to batched dense GEMMs with regular GPU memory access.

**What's new:** The approach scales at O(N) for a fixed block size and sustains 60–120 fps (8–16 ms/solve) on GPU across a wide range of problem sizes — keeping solve time below the real-time budget line up to thousands of unknowns, where classical preconditioners typically fall off.

**Why it matters:** Real-time physics/deformation solves underpin cloth, soft-body, and collision simulation in interactive 3D and XR content (including simulation-ready assets like the OmniFabric garments above). A GPU-native neural preconditioner that holds real-time budgets at higher unknown counts widens what's feasible for live physically-based XR content.

## 5. NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Nano Omni — Multimodal Perception Model

**Source:** [NVIDIA blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nemotron-3-nano-omni-multimodal-ai-agents/) · [NVIDIA developer blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-nemotron-3-nano-omni-powers-multimodal-agent-reasoning-in-a-single-efficient-open-model/) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/nemotron-3-nano-omni-multimodal-intelligence)
**Date:** September 2026

**What it is:** A 30B-A3B hybrid Mamba-Transformer mixture-of-experts model that natively handles text, image, video, and audio in one network, positioned as a multimodal "perception sub-agent" for agentic AI. It supports visual grounding, OCR, document/chart/table understanding, GUI understanding, and video/audio understanding without separate task-specific perception models.

**What's new:** Folding vision and audio grounding into a single efficient MoE (rather than chaining separate detectors/OCR/segmentation models) tops multiple leaderboards for document intelligence and video/audio understanding while cutting inference cost, per NVIDIA's reported benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** A single open, efficient grounding/perception backbone that covers visual grounding plus document and GUI understanding is useful as a drop-in front-end for scene-understanding and semantic-labeling stages of 3D/XR pipelines (e.g., tagging captured objects, reading in-scene text, grounding language queries against a reconstructed scene) without stitching together multiple specialist models.

## 6. Meta Connect 2026 — Meta VR Glasses Unveiled

**Source:** [UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/meta-vr-glasses-officially-announced-connect-2026/) · [Road to VR hands-on](https://roadtovr.com/meta-vr-glass-hands-onconnect-2026/) · [Meta announcement](https://www.meta.com/blog/meta-vr-glasses-announcement-meta-connect/) · [AR Insider weekly recap](https://arinsider.co/2026/09/25/ai-xr-beats-tech-takes-over-meta-charms-qualcomm-gets-personal/)
**Date:** Announced September 23, 2026 at Meta Connect; shipping Spring 2027; still the top story in this week's XR press roundups (through Sep 28)

**What it is:** A ~100 g VR/MR glasses form factor (not a bulky headset) with compute, battery, and storage offloaded to a separate puck connected via an optical tether. Specs include magnesium-alloy pancake-lens optics, 5K combined display (5.5MP per eye, 37 PPD) at up to 120Hz, full-color passthrough at 26 PPD (vs. 18 PPD on Quest 3), a four-camera eye-tracking system, hand tracking, and optional Touch Plus controllers, with Dolby Vision/Atmos support. Priced at $1,299.99.

**What's new:** Splitting compute/battery from the optics into a tethered puck is Meta's answer to the weight ceiling that has kept standalone MR headsets bulky; the passthrough pixel density jump (18→26 PPD) is a meaningful step toward mixed-reality passthrough quality that's usable for extended sessions.

**Why it matters:** This is the most concrete signal yet that "glasses-weight" MR is becoming a real shipping category rather than a lab demo, which directly affects what kind of 3D content (splat/mesh density, texture streaming budget) is worth authoring for near-term consumer MR hardware, and raises the passthrough-quality bar that scene-reconstruction and world-locking pipelines need to match.
