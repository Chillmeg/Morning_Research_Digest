## 1. Scene-Agnostic Object-Centric Representation Learning for 3D Gaussian Splatting
**Source:** [arXiv 2604.09045](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09045v2) via [DailyArXiv #300](https://github.com/lizixuan-up/DailyArXiv/issues/300) · **Date:** Oct 6, 2026 (3DMV workshop, CVPR 2026)

**What it is:** Learns object-centric representations over 3D Gaussian scenes that transfer across scenes rather than being fit per scene.
**What's new / why it matters:** Per-scene semantic features are a bottleneck for editing and segmentation. Scene-agnostic object embeddings could give reusable object-level handles in splat scenes (selection, removal, composition) for XR content pipelines.

## 2. PocketSplat — Mobile Gaussian Reconstruction via World-Space Latent Allocation
**Source:** [Search listing, Oct 5 2026](https://github.com/lizixuan-up/DailyArXiv/issues/300) (see also [radiancefields.com/papers](https://radiancefields.com/papers)) · **Date:** Oct 5, 2026

**What it is:** A mobile-targeted Gaussian reconstruction method that allocates latent capacity in world space.
**Relevance:** On-device capture and reconstruction is key for headset/phone XR workflows. Check memory and latency numbers against feed-forward methods.

## 3. Budgeted-GS — Real-Time Large-Scale Gaussian Splatting via Factoring LOD
**Date:** Oct 5, 2026 · **Source:** [radiancefields.com/papers](https://radiancefields.com/papers)

**What it is:** Level-of-detail factoring that bounds the rendering budget for large scenes.
**Relevance:** Fixed-budget rendering is what city-scale or room-scale splat viewing on standalone XR hardware needs.

## 4. ManifoldSplat — Language-Guided Semantic Shape Editing of 3D Gaussian Head Avatars
**Date:** Oct 5, 2026 · **Source:** [DailyArXiv](https://github.com/lizixuan-up/DailyArXiv/issues/300)

Text-driven shape edits on Gaussian head avatars; relevant to telepresence avatars. Related: [Eurographics 2026 relightable Gaussian avatars](https://team.inria.fr/virtus/eurographics-2026-paper-bridging-gaussian-splatting-and-texture-mapping-for-relightable-and-editable-gaussian-avatars/).

## 5. SCION — Scene Composition with Instanced Neural Primitives
**Date:** Oct 5, 2026

Instancing of neural primitives for composing scenes, which suggests memory savings and editability for repeated objects. Worth reading for 3D modeling workflows.

## 6. MEGA — Object-Level Mesh Extraction from 3DGS via Spatial Visual Distillation
**Date:** Oct 2, 2026

Extracts per-object meshes from splats, bridging Gaussian captures and mesh-based tools (physics, CAD, game engines).

## 7. Robustness, metrics and data (Oct 2, 2026)
- **EvenSplat:** coupled 2D-3D decomposition for exposure/illumination variation (in-the-wild capture).
- **GS-PQM:** parameter-domain quality metric for compressed Gaussian splatting, a useful benchmark tool for compression work.
- **3DROID:** renderable 3D Gaussian dataset with measured per-scene reliability.
- Also: *Dirichlet Splatting* (wave-based inverse problems), *FactorSplat* (medical volume rendering).

## 8. Streaming 3D Reconstruction
**Source:** [Revisiting Local Context for Long-Horizon Streaming 3D Reconstruction (arXiv 2608.27529)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.27529); NeurIPS 2026 paper "Rethinking the State Update Gate for Long-Sequence Recurrent 3D Reconstruction"; [STAC cache compression, CVPR 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.27529)

**Trend:** Memory-bounded, long-horizon streaming feed-forward reconstruction (DUSt3R/VGGT-style) is the active front: state gating and cache compression for long videos.
