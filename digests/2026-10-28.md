## 1. SpAItial Echo-3

**Source:** [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/spaitial-echo-3-turns-iphone-scans-into-3d-worlds) · **Date:** Oct 27, 2026

**What it is:** A world model that converts a few minutes of iPhone capture into a real-world-scale Gaussian splat scene, processed in the cloud.

**What's new:** Moves from per-scene optimisation toward a learned, cloud-hosted capture-to-splat service with metric scale.

**Relevance:** Useful baseline for casual-capture pipelines and XR scene import; worth benchmarking against your own 3DGS/feed-forward reconstructions for scale accuracy and completeness.

## 2. Nuke 18.0 Open Beta

**Source:** [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/) · **Date:** Oct 27, 2026

**What it is:** Foundry's compositor adds splat viewport optimisation, a FieldThreshold node for procedural splat isolation, and splat selection/snapping.

**What's new:** Splats become first-class, procedurally editable data in a mainstream VFX tool.

**Relevance:** Indicates the direction for splat editing/cleanup workflows; relevant if you need to hand off captures to production pipelines.

## 3. SuperSplat Editor 3.7

**Source:** [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/) · **Date:** Oct 26, 2026

**What it is:** Adds 24 WebMCP tools letting AI agents select, edit, transform and export Gaussian splats, with shared undo and radial sorting.

**What's new:** First notable splat editor exposing an agent-facing (MCP) interface.

**Relevance:** Enables scripted/LLM-driven splat cleanup and dataset preparation.

## 4. Framestore 4D Gaussian Splatting on *Superman*

**Source:** [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/) · **Date:** Oct 23, 2026

**What it is:** Talk detailing a pipeline from a 192-camera, 88-second take trained in Postshot to per-shot PLY files in Houdini.

**What's new:** A concrete large-scale production use of 4D splatting with documented capture/training/handoff stages.

**Relevance:** Real-world reference for multi-camera dynamic capture scale and practical bottlenecks.

## 5. Blender 5.3 Native Gaussian Splatting

**Source:** [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/splatgen-trains-and-relights-gaussian-splats-inside-the-blender-5-3-alpha) · **Date:** Release expected Nov 10, 2026

**What it is:** Native PLY and SPZ v4 splat support across Workbench, EEVEE and Cycles; the SplatGen add-on trains and relights splats inside the 5.3 alpha.

**Relevance:** Free, open DCC support for splats lowers the barrier for mixing splats with meshes and relighting experiments.
